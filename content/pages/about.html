--- title: About somewhere.ph description: Who we are and how we research our Philippines travel guides. ---

Some of the best memories are made on a boat deck at sunrise, a plate of fresh mango in hand and an island coming into view. somewhere.ph is a guide for making more of those memories, whether it is your first trip to the Philippines or your first trip back in twenty years.

We love the old Philippines as much as the new one: the jeepneys and sari-sari stores, the long family lunches and the pier where everyone waves goodbye. You will find both in our guides, alongside the practical details that make a trip easy today.

How we write our guides

Every guide mixes a little history and a lot of practical advice.

  • We cover the practical details: flights and ferries, the best months for each island, where to stay and realistic costs.
  • Every guide shows the date it was last reviewed, and we revisit guides regularly.
  • Entry rules, fees and boat schedules change, so we link to official sources and ask you to confirm before you travel.

Share your memories

Do you have an old photo, a postcard or a story from one of the places we write about? We would love to hear it, and with your permission we may share it in a guide. Send it to hello@somewhere.ph.
