Every great trip starts the same way: a map spread on the kitchen table, a pen and somebody saying, "What if we went here?" These itineraries are our version of that map. They are built for a first or second trip and leave room for the moments you can't plan, like a long lunch that turns into a sunset or a boat captain who insists on one more stop.

3. Easy first-timer: Boracay and Bohol

Boracay has the most developed tourism infrastructure in the country, with White Beach, sunset sailing and plenty of restaurants. Fly to Caticlan (the closest airport) or Kalibo, then take the short boat across. Pair five or six days in Boracay with four days on Bohol for countryside and wildlife.

Tips for any route

  • Book domestic flights early for December, Holy Week and summer school holidays.
  • Keep one buffer day before your international flight in case a boat is cancelled.
  • Check baggage allowances on small domestic planes, which can be strict.

Then and now

A generation ago, island hopping meant reading schedules chalked on a board at the pier and hoping the boat came. Travelers kept journals, mailed postcards that arrived after they got home and shot just one roll of film per island, so every photo counted. You can plan everything from your phone now, but try leaving one day empty. Some of the best memories still come from the days nobody planned.
